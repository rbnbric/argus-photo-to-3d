**Title**

Argus | From Photos to Inspectable 3D — Nebius × NVIDIA Demo

**Description**

Argus turns a photo set into a 3D asset you can inspect against its source. This 2:27 demo follows a twelve-view fox reconstruction through camera-matched renders, material layers, residual heatmaps, and a real Nebius GPU diagnostic.

Try the interactive demo: https://rbnbric.github.io/argus-photo-to-3d/
Explore the real-photo material studies: https://rbnbric.github.io/argus-photo-to-3d/surface-examples.html
Source and reports: https://github.com/rbnbric/argus-photo-to-3d

What ran where: the fox mesh and textures were reconstructed locally. An NVIDIA H100 Nebius AI Cloud Serverless Job processed twelve saved photo/render pairs and returned twenty-four verified heatmaps. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super through Nebius Token Factory reviewed measured results and recommended a next material test. Argus's deterministic gates still mark the material and captured-light fit as review candidates.

Fox source photographs: John Davies, Photogrammetry-examples/Fox, CC BY-SA 4.0. The photographs were resized and recompressed; Argus made masks, rendered comparisons, model/material adaptations, gallery plates, and this video. The physical sculpture's maker is unknown. Source: https://github.com/john-davies/Photogrammetry-examples/tree/master/Fox | License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The linked WIP studies use “Community Building (2014) 8247” by Grand Canyon National Park and “my office” by Robbie Sproule, both CC BY 2.0. Their credits and adaptation notes are in the repository. The video does not present them as validated PBR capture.
